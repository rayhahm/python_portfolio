# ServiceTitan Mobile App Basics (Technicians)

> ⚠️ Starter template — verify against your live ServiceTitan account and
> correct any menu names/button labels that have changed, then delete this note.

## Your daily queue

- Open the app and check **Today**/**My Jobs** to see the day's dispatched
  jobs in order.
- Tap a job to see customer info, address, job history, and notes before
  heading out.

## On the way and on-site

1. Tap **On My Way** when you leave for the job — this can trigger an
   automatic notification/text to the customer.
2. Tap **Start Job** (or similar) when you arrive, so the office and
   customer see accurate timing.
3. Use the job screen to view equipment history, add photos, and take
   notes as you diagnose/work.

## Estimates, invoices, and payment on mobile

- Build or present an Estimate from the job screen; the customer can
  approve/sign on your device.
- When work is done, complete the Invoice and collect payment on the same
  screen (see the Estimates, Invoices, and Payments articles).

## Wrapping up a job

1. Add a summary of the work performed and any recommendations.
2. Attach photos (before/after) if required by your company's SOP.
3. Mark the job **Complete** — this is what allows the office to close out
   payroll and reporting correctly, so don't skip it.

## Asking ServiceTitan's own AI assistant (Atlas)

ServiceTitan has a built-in AI assistant called **Atlas** inside the
platform, which can answer feature/workflow questions and point you to
Help Center articles without leaving the app. Use this bot for company-
specific "how do we do X here" questions, and Atlas (or
help.servicetitan.com) for general ServiceTitan feature questions.
