# Adding and Managing a Customer

> ⚠️ Starter template — verify against your live ServiceTitan account and
> correct any menu names/button labels that have changed, then delete this note.

## Adding a new Customer

1. Click **+ New** and choose **Customer** (or start a new Job and create
   the customer inline when it isn't found in search).
2. Enter the customer's name, phone number(s), and email.
3. Add at least one **Location** (service address) — a customer can have
   multiple locations (e.g., a property management company).
4. Set the customer type (Residential / Commercial) and any relevant tags
   or lead source.
5. Save the record.

## Avoiding duplicate customers

- Always search by phone number first — it's the most reliable way to find
  an existing customer before creating a new one.
- Merge duplicate customer records through the admin tools if you find two
  entries for the same person/property (ask an office admin if you're not
  sure how).

## Customer and Location details worth keeping current

- Gate codes, pet warnings, or access notes (use the **Notes** field on the
  Location).
- Equipment installed at the location (for service history and
  maintenance reminders).
- Membership/contract status, which affects pricing and invoicing.
