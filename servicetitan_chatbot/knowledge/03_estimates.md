# Creating an Estimate

## When to use an Estimate

Use an Estimate when you need customer approval on pricing before doing the
work — for example, a repair option comparison ("good/better/best") or a
system replacement quote.

## Creating an Estimate from a Job

1. Open the relevant Job.
2. Go to the **Estimates** tab and click **+ New Estimate** (or **Add
   Estimate**).
3. Add line items from the **Pricebook** (services, equipment, materials) or
   enter a custom line item.
4. Optionally create multiple estimate options (e.g., Repair vs. Replace)
   so the customer can compare and choose one.
5. Review pricing, taxes, and any discounts, then **Save**.

## Presenting and getting approval

- Technicians typically present estimates to the customer on their mobile
  device or tablet using the ServiceTitan Mobile app's Estimate view.
- The customer can approve/sign digitally on the spot; approved estimates
  can be converted directly into scheduled work or an invoice.

## Converting an Estimate to a Job/Invoice

- Once approved, use **Convert to Job** (if the work isn't scheduled yet) or
  proceed straight to invoicing if the work is already done, pulling the
  approved line items into the invoice automatically.
