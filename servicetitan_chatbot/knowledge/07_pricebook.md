# Pricebook Basics

> ⚠️ Starter template — verify against your live ServiceTitan account and
> correct any menu names/button labels that have changed, then delete this note.

## What the Pricebook is

The **Pricebook** is the company's catalog of sellable services, materials,
equipment, and labor — with set pricing, cost, and margin built in. When you
add a line item to an Estimate or Invoice from the Pricebook, the price is
already calculated consistently instead of being typed by hand each time.

## Finding a Pricebook item

1. On an Estimate or Invoice, click **+ Add Item** (or similar).
2. Search by item name, code, or category (e.g., "capacitor", "AC tune-up").
3. Select the item — it will pull in the description, price, and any
   default warranty/taxable settings.

## When something isn't in the Pricebook

- Don't just type a random custom price for a real recurring service or
  part — flag it to your office admin/manager so it gets added to the
  Pricebook properly (keeps pricing consistent across the whole team).
- For a true one-off, a custom line item can be added manually, but this
  should be the exception, not the norm.

## Who maintains the Pricebook

- Pricebook edits (adding new items, changing prices) are normally
  restricted to admins/managers, not every field technician — check your
  account's permission settings if you need edit access.
