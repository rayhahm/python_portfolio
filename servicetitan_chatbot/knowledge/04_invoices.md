# Creating and Sending an Invoice

> ⚠️ Starter template — verify against your live ServiceTitan account and
> correct any menu names/button labels that have changed, then delete this note.

## Creating an Invoice

1. Open the completed Job.
2. Go to the **Invoice** tab. If line items were already added via an
   approved Estimate, they should already be pulled in.
3. Add or adjust line items from the **Pricebook** for any additional
   materials, labor, or services performed.
4. Apply any discounts, membership pricing, or financing options that
   apply to this customer.
5. Review the total, then click **Save** / **Complete Invoice**.

## Sending the Invoice to the customer

- Technicians can email or text the invoice directly from the mobile app
  on-site, or office staff can send it from the desktop view.
- Use **Print/Email/Text Invoice** to deliver it in the customer's
  preferred format.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Forgetting to mark the job **Completed** before invoicing, which can
  affect payroll and reporting.
- Leaving out warranty or membership discounts that should auto-apply —
  double check the customer's Membership tab before finalizing.
