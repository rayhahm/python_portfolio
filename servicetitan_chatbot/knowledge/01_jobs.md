# Creating and Managing a Job

> ⚠️ Starter template — verify against your live ServiceTitan account and
> correct any menu names/button labels that have changed, then delete this note.

## What a Job is

In ServiceTitan, a **Job** is the core work record: it links a customer,
a service location, the technician(s) assigned, the scheduled time, the
work performed, and the resulting estimate/invoice. Almost everything else
(scheduling, dispatch, invoicing) happens on or from a Job.

## Creating a new Job

1. Click the **+ New** (or **Add**) button, usually in the top toolbar or
   from the Call Booking / Dispatch board.
2. Search for the customer by name, phone, or address. If they don't exist
   yet, create a new Customer and Location first (see the Customers article).
3. Choose the **Business Unit** and **Job Type** (e.g., Service Call,
   Installation, Maintenance) — this drives which technicians and pricebook
   items are available.
4. Add a short description of the work requested (the "Job Summary").
5. Save the job — this creates the job record, which you can then schedule.

## Finding an existing Job

- Use the top search bar and type the customer name, phone number, or job
  number.
- From the Dispatch board, jobs appear as cards/blocks you can click to open.

## Job statuses

Jobs move through statuses such as **Unscheduled → Scheduled → Dispatched →
In Progress → Completed → Invoiced/Closed**. The exact status list can be
customized per company; ask your ServiceTitan admin if your account uses
different names.
