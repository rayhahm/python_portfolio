# Collecting a Payment

> ⚠️ Starter template — verify against your live ServiceTitan account and
> correct any menu names/button labels that have changed, then delete this note.

## Collecting payment on-site (technician)

1. From the completed Job's Invoice screen in the ServiceTitan Mobile app,
   tap **Collect Payment**.
2. Choose the payment method: credit/debit card (tap-to-pay or manual
   entry), check, cash, or financing.
3. For card payments, use the connected card reader if the company has one,
   or enter the card manually.
4. Confirm the amount and get the customer's signature/confirmation, then
   submit.

## Collecting payment from the office

1. Open the Job or Invoice from the desktop view.
2. Click **Add Payment**.
3. Select the payment method and amount, then save. For card payments this
   usually goes through the integrated payment processor.

## Refunds and adjustments

- Refunds and payment corrections typically need to go through the office
  (not the field technician) — check with your admin on who is authorized
  to process refunds in your account.

## Financing

- If the customer wants financing, use the **Financing** option on the
  Estimate/Invoice screen to send an application through the integrated
  financing partner, rather than collecting payment directly.
