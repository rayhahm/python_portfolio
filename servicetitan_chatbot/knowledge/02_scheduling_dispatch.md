# Scheduling and Dispatching a Technician

> ⚠️ Starter template — verify against your live ServiceTitan account and
> correct any menu names/button labels that have changed, then delete this note.

## Scheduling a Job

1. Open the **Dispatch Board** (usually in the left navigation).
2. Find the unscheduled job in the "Unscheduled" panel, or open the job and
   click **Schedule**.
3. Drag the job onto the technician's row at the desired time slot, or pick
   a technician and arrival window from the schedule dialog.
4. Confirm the arrival window — the customer typically gets an automatic
   text/email confirmation and "on my way" notifications when configured.

## Dispatching (sending the job to the technician)

1. Once scheduled, click the job card on the Dispatch Board.
2. Click **Dispatch** to push the job to the technician's mobile app.
3. The technician receives a notification and the job appears in their
   ServiceTitan Mobile app queue for the day.

## Rescheduling or reassigning

- Drag the job card to a different time slot or technician row on the
  Dispatch Board, or open the job and use **Reschedule** / **Reassign**.
- Always leave a note in the job's activity log explaining why it moved,
  so office staff and the technician have context.

## Tips

- Use the **Unscheduled** and **Today** views to make sure nothing is left
  unassigned at the end of the day.
- Color-coded technician status (available, dispatched, en route, working,
  done) on the Dispatch Board shows real-time crew status at a glance.
